🔒 Privacy

What this site collects

  • An anonymous random ID (cryptographically random, stored in a first-party cookie) so I can tell a returning browser from a new one.
  • Visit events: timestamp, page path, referrer when your browser sends one, device category (mobile/tablet/desktop), browser family, and whether you arrived from an in-app browser.
  • Coarse technical context: language list, time zone, screen-size bucket, connection type.

What it never collects

  • No names, emails, phone numbers, or account identifiers of any kind.
  • No cross-site tracking, no third-party pixels, no ad networks.
  • No exact IP addresses (only a salted one-way hash for abuse analysis).
  • No precise device fingerprinting.

Important honesty note

The anonymous ID never reveals your identity. In my dashboard, matching a returning visit is labeled PROBABILISTIC (same browser, probably) or DETERMINISTIC only for my own cookie ID — which is anonymous by design.

Your control

Clearing cookies or browsing privately ends visit recognition. No login is required to browse this site.
